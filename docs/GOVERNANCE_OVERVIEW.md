# AI WELLMAYBE — Governance Overview

AI ratings become less useful if commercial pressure, tool ownership, incomplete evidence or publication incentives can silently change what a result means.

AI WELLMAYBE therefore treats governance as part of rating integrity, not as a separate marketing appendix.

## Public governance themes

### Evidence and provenance

Material evidence should retain enough provenance, scope and timing context to understand where it came from and what it can support.

Authentic provenance does not automatically make content true.

### Independence and anti-capture

Payment, ownership claims, partnerships, institutional interest, tool ownership or participation in methodology work should not automatically confer a better result, a veto over evidence or authority over the methodology.

### Separation of statements

Owner claims, third-party scores, regulator statements, external assessments and AI WELLMAYBE / AIRR conclusions should remain distinguishable.

### Correction without historical erasure

Confirmed factual errors should be correctable. Challenges should be reviewable. New versions and materially new evidence may require reassessment.

Correction, challenge and reassessment are not the same process.

### Publication is a separate decision

Possessing evidence does not mean that all evidence should be public.

Private technical material, sensitive evidence, personal data, credentials and exploit details require separate handling and publication decisions.

### Public and internal layers are intentionally different

The public layer explains how to interpret outputs and what principles constrain the process.

The internal layer may contain operational controls, registers, test design, security-sensitive evidence handling and implementation material that is inappropriate to publish.

The existence of an internal governance category should not be read as a claim that every possible control is already deployed in production.

## Why the boundary matters

A credible evaluation system needs enough transparency to be interpretable and challengeable, while retaining enough non-public structure to resist trivial gaming, protect sensitive evidence and avoid exposing customer or security-sensitive information.

AI WELLMAYBE intentionally treats those goals as complementary rather than contradictory.
