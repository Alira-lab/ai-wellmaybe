# AI WELLMAYBE Diagnostics for AI Agents

AI agents combine models, tools, instructions, permissions, external services, persistent state and changing runtime conditions.

A useful diagnostic view therefore needs to answer more than whether a model produced a correct text response.

## What diagnostics are intended to clarify

At a public level, AI WELLMAYBE Diagnostics is intended to help distinguish:

- the exact system or rated configuration being discussed;
- declared behavior from observed behavior;
- an observation from a confirmed finding;
- a finding from a still-open exposure or consequence;
- a current issue from a historical issue that has been remediated and retested;
- an assessed surface from a surface that remains unknown or not assessed;
- evidence from the conclusion drawn from that evidence.

## Diagnostic Card concept

The Diagnostic Card concept is intended to translate technical assessment state into practical questions such as:

- what happened;
- why it matters;
- what consequence may be possible;
- what remains uncertain;
- what kind of safe outcome or remediation is required;
- whether retesting supports closure;
- whether the evidence is still current.

Different audiences may need different levels of detail, but presentation should not silently change the underlying meaning.

## Agent-specific complexity

Agents can introduce additional questions around tool use, authority, delegation, persistent state, external evidence, changing configurations and consequential actions.

This public repository intentionally does not publish internal test catalogs, exploit scenarios, routing rules or enforcement logic used to investigate those questions.
