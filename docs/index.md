# AI WELLMAYBE

**Independent AI ratings · Registry · Diagnostics**

AI WELLMAYBE is the company/platform brand used by Alira-lab for evidence-backed evaluation of AI systems, AI agents and automated processes.

AI Risk Ratings (AIRR) is the underlying technical methodology/system.

## Public product family

- **Ratings** — bounded, evidence-backed assessment results.
- **Registry** — searchable subject/profile and assessment-history concepts.
- **Diagnostics** — findings, limitations, remediation and reassessment views.
- **Edge** — future observation/protection product direction built around evidence-backed state and local policy.

## Public disclosure boundary

AI WELLMAYBE publishes enough methodology and governance context to make public outputs interpretable.

It does not publish hidden tests, exact thresholds or weights, exploit sequences, anti-gaming mechanisms, private evidence, internal adjudication routing or security-sensitive implementation details.

This separation is deliberate.
