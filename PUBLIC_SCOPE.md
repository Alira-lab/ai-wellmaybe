# AI WELLMAYBE — Public Scope

**Current as of:** 20 October 2026

AI WELLMAYBE is the company/platform brand used by Alira-lab for independent AI ratings, registry, diagnostics, evaluation software and related research.

AI Risk Ratings (AIRR) is the underlying technical methodology/system currently used for evidence-backed rating and diagnostic work.

## What the public project describes

The public record may describe:

- the purpose and product family of AI WELLMAYBE;
- high-level principles for evidence-backed AI ratings and diagnostics;
- the distinction between an AI subject and the exact version, deployment or configuration assessed;
- the role of scope, evidence, confidence, freshness and limitations;
- public registry/profile concepts;
- correction, challenge, reassessment and historical supersession principles;
- independence and conflict-management principles;
- high-level data, evidence, privacy and security boundaries;
- areas of product and service activity.

## What the public project does not disclose

Public documentation intentionally does not publish operational or security-sensitive internals, including:

- hidden tests, probes, fixtures or adversarial scenarios;
- exact scoring weights, thresholds, calibration logic or anti-gaming rules;
- exploit payloads, exploit chains or restricted technical evidence;
- private assessment data, customer architecture, credentials or secrets;
- internal adjudication routing or reviewer-assignment logic;
- internal implementation maps, dependency sequences or control-state registers;
- security-sensitive infrastructure, access-control, provider or deployment details.

The absence of those details from the public repository should not be interpreted as an indication that a specific internal mechanism exists, is deployed or has any particular implementation status.

## Interpretation boundary

A public AI WELLMAYBE or AIRR result is intended to remain bounded to the identified subject, rated version/configuration, capability, assessment scope, evidence available at the relevant time and the stated limitations.

Assessment depth is not a universal safety grade.

Unknown is not the same as safe, and unknown is not the same as guilty.

Evidence is not automatically a rating. A rating is not automatically policy, permission or enforcement.

## Product-status discipline

Public documentation may describe planned product families or future-facing concepts. Description of a product area does not itself claim that every related feature is deployed, generally available or production-ready.

The public repository is a brand, methodology and product-scope record. It is not the source repository for AIRR internal implementation.
