Public methodology posture

AI WELLMAYBE does not treat assessment depth as a universal safety grade. A meaningful result remains bounded to the identified system or rated configuration, capability, scope, available evidence, confidence, freshness and material limitations.

Evidence is not automatically a rating. A rating is not automatically policy, permission or enforcement.

Deliberate disclosure boundary

Public documentation is intended to make outputs interpretable and challengeable without publishing the internal machinery needed to reproduce, game or bypass the evaluation process.

The public surface does not disclose hidden tests, exact thresholds or weights, exploit sequences, anti-gaming mechanisms, private evidence, internal adjudication routing or security-sensitive implementation details.
