# AI WELLMAYBE Ratings

An AI rating is useful only if the reader can tell **what was rated, under what scope, with what evidence and how current that evidence is**.

AI WELLMAYBE Ratings is intended to provide evidence-backed decision support for bounded AI systems, AI agents and automated processes.

## A rating is not a universal label

The same product name can represent different versions, deployments, routing, tools, permissions, configurations and operating environments.

For that reason, a rating should not automatically transfer from one rated configuration to another.

## A rating is more than one number

A public rating may use concise summaries, but the underlying meaning is expected to remain tied to:

- identity and rated version/configuration;
- assessed capability and scope;
- evidence and its limitations;
- findings or unresolved uncertainty;
- confidence and freshness;
- material changes and reassessment history.

A numerical compatibility field, where one exists, should not be treated as the entire truth model or as proof that one AI system is universally “better” than another.

## External evidence remains external evidence

A scanner result, vendor attestation, benchmark, model output, certification or third-party assessment can be relevant evidence.

It does not automatically become an AI WELLMAYBE / AIRR conclusion merely because it is available.

## The expected public discipline

AI WELLMAYBE aims to make a result useful for practical decision support without turning it into an absolute certification, blanket permission or guarantee of safety.

That means preserving scope, uncertainty and freshness instead of hiding them behind a single green label.
