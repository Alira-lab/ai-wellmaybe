# AI WELLMAYBE

**AI WELLMAYBE** is the current company/platform brand used by **Alira-lab** for independent AI ratings, registry, diagnostics, evaluation software and related research.

**Public brand record:** 20 October 2026  
**Current brand family:** AI WELLMAYBE Ratings · AI WELLMAYBE Registry · AI WELLMAYBE Diagnostics · AI WELLMAYBE Edge

## What AI WELLMAYBE is for

AI WELLMAYBE develops evidence-backed tools and services for understanding AI systems, AI agents and automated processes.

The public product direction includes:

- ratings and assessment results tied to specific AI systems, capabilities, scope and available evidence;
- searchable registry/profile information and assessment history;
- diagnostics that distinguish observed findings, evidence limits, remediation and reassessment status;
- software and machine-readable data for interpreting current AI-system status;
- research, education and publications concerning AI evaluation, risk and responsible deployment;
- future observation and protection software built on evidence-backed status and local policy.

AI WELLMAYBE does not use assessment depth as a universal claim that an AI system is “safe.” Results are intended to remain bounded to the system, version, capability, scope and evidence actually assessed.

## Product family

### AI WELLMAYBE Ratings
Evidence-backed ratings and assessment results for bounded AI systems and capabilities.

### AI WELLMAYBE Registry
Searchable subject/profile, assessment-state and rating-history surfaces.

### AI WELLMAYBE Diagnostics
Findings, consequences, remediation/retest status and diagnostic views.

### AI WELLMAYBE Edge
A future observation/protection surface intended to consume evidence-backed state and local policy. Listing it here describes the product family; it does not represent a claim that every future function is already deployed.

The current brand scope includes product and service areas commonly associated with the following Nice Classification fields.

### Class 9 — software and downloadable digital products

- downloadable and recorded computer software and software applications;
- software platforms for AI-system and AI-agent assessment, monitoring, diagnostics and analysis;

### Class 41 — education, training and publications

- education and training relating to AI systems, AI agents, AI risk and evaluation;

- publication of educational texts, research summaries, methodology explainers and reports concerning AI evaluation and ratings;
- online educational information and materials concerning interpretation of AI assessment results.

### Class 42 — technology, research, testing and SaaS

- scientific and technological research, analysis, testing and evaluation concerning AI systems and AI agents;
- technological evaluation, testing, inspection and quality-control services for software and AI-enabled systems;
- SaaS, PaaS and AIaaS for AI assessment, diagnostics, monitoring, ratings, registry/profile and machine-readable decision support;
- design and development of computer software and software systems;
- technological consultancy concerning AI evaluation, evidence, software security, operational risk and deployment controls;
- non-downloadable software and API services for assessment workflows, ratings, diagnostics, profiles and evidence/history views.

## Technical continuity

**AI Risk Ratings (AIRR)** is the underlying technical methodology/system currently used for evidence-backed rating and diagnostic work.

The public AI WELLMAYBE brand and the AIRR technical layer are intentionally separated: brand evolution does not by itself rename stable technical identifiers, assessment records, schemas or historical evidence.
